## Timely topic ideas
- **"My bank is sending someone to pick up my card."** On Oct 20, Hamilton (Ontario) police warned about a scam in three steps. First, a fake "phone/internet provider discount" call. Next, a fake bank call saying your account is compromised. Finally, a courier comes to collect your cards, and the caller talks you into giving your PIN. Police advice: banks never send couriers for cards or PINs, and a caller who knows your details isn't proof they're real. Hang up and call the number on your card ([CHCH, Oct 20](https://www.chch.com/chch-news/hamilton-police-warns-public-of-new-scam-targeting-seniors/)). The same pattern keeps showing up in UK and Canadian police warnings ([Action Fraud](https://www.actionfraud.police.uk/news/police-warn-people-of-new-courier-scam), [Calgary](https://newsroom.calgary.ca/police-warn-of-bank-card-scam-involving-courier-service/)), so it suits a Short for your international viewers. Suggested one-line rule: *"Your bank will never send someone to your door for your card or PIN."*
